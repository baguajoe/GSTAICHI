Bulk import articles

Choose several Word files at once (.docx or the older .doc). Each one becomes a members-only article by Vincent Chu, converted the same way as "Upload an article" in the Teacher Studio.

  • The title comes from the file name: Chapter 7 Tai Chi and Tai Chi Chuan.doc becomes Tai Chi and Tai Chi Chuan. They are listed in chapter order.
  • A file whose title is already an article is skipped, so nothing is ever added twice or overwritten.
  • Old .doc files keep only the words and paragraphs. For italics and Word's list numbers, save them as .docx first.
